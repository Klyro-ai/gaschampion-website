What I Can Help With

From a dripping tap to a full boiler installation, here's what I do.

  1. Boiler Installation

    Supply and fit of new energy-efficient boilers from trusted brands like Worcester Bosch and Vaillant. Includes full system flush and setup.

  2. Boiler Repairs

    Quick and reliable fault diagnosis and repair. I carry the most common parts in my van, so many fixes are done on the first visit.

  3. Annual Boiler Servicing

    Keep your boiler safe and running efficiently with a thorough annual service. Helps prevent breakdowns and keeps your warranty valid.

  4. Central Heating Systems

    Full system design and installation. New radiators, pipework, smart thermostats, and zone controls tailored to your home.

  5. General Plumbing

    Bathroom installations, tap replacements, leak repairs, and general plumbing maintenance throughout your home.

  6. Landlord Gas Safety Certificates

    CP12 certificates for landlords, letting agents, and housing associations. Same-day certification with competitive rates.
